The runtime statically includes whisper/ggml and the common audio decoder
utilities. The following original notices apply to their respective components.
This inventory describes the pinned arm64 CPU + Apple Accelerate build. Metal,
KleidiAI, llamafile, curl and FFmpeg are not linked into that build. System
frameworks are supplied by macOS rather than redistributed with this notice.
If the runtime or its build configuration changes, review this inventory again.
